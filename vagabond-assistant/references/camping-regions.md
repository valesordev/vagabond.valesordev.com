# Camping Regions

Brian is based in University Heights, San Diego. Primary target regions below.

---

## Mojave National Preserve

**Drive from San Diego**: ~3.5–4hr via I-15 N
**Season**: Oct–Apr optimal; May–Sep brutal (110°F+ possible)
**Permit**: No permit required for dispersed camping (14-day limit)
**Cell service**: Minimal; Verizon/ATT spotty near Kelso; Starlink essential

### Key Areas
| Area | Character | Road Type |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Kelso Dunes | Iconic dunes, moderate hiking | Paved to trailhead |
| Hole-in-the-Wall | Slot canyon hikes, established camping | Paved |
| Mid Hills | Higher elevation (5,600ft), cooler, piñon-juniper | Paved + dirt |
| Cima Dome | Joshua tree forest, dispersed camping | Dirt roads |
| Providence Mountains | Limestone caves (Mitchell Caverns), remote | Dirt |

### Planning Notes
- Water: NO reliable water sources in backcountry — carry full capacity (7gal minimum)
- Flash floods: monsoon season Jul–Sep; avoid washes overnight
- Roads: Most accessible with stock 4Runner; some roads warrant aired-down tires
- Fire restrictions: Check current restrictions at nps.gov/moja
- Wildlife: Mojave rattlesnakes, desert tortoise (protected), coyotes

### Dispersed Camping Rules
- 14-day stay limit; must move 25mi after limit
- No dispersed camping within ¼ mile of water sources, roads, or developed sites
- Pack out all waste; no ground fires during restrictions

## Eastern Sierra Corridor

**Drive from San Diego**: ~5–6hr via US-395 N
**Season**: Jun–Oct (passes open); Nov–May high passes closed
**Cell**: AT&T decent in towns (Bishop, Mammoth, Lone Pine); backcountry dead

### Sub-Regions
| Area | Character | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Owens Valley | Base camp corridor, fishing (Owens River) | US-395 spine |
| Bishop Creek Canyon | Trout fishing, high alpine lakes | Paved to South Lake/Lake Sabrina; dirt above |
| Rock Creek | Less crowded, excellent dispersed sites | Paved + dirt mix |
| June Lake Loop | Scenic, fishing, good shoulder-season access | Paved |
| Mammoth Lakes | Recreation hub; crowded in summer | Paved |
| Alabama Hills | BLM, iconic geology, no permit dispersed | Dirt roads, accessible |
| Cottonwood Creek / Portal | Southern Sierra access, solitude | High clearance useful |

### Planning Notes
- **Inyo NF Permit System**: Some wilderness zones require permits (lottery or walk-up) — check fs.usda.gov/inyo
- **Water**: Generally available (filter required); pack backup
- **Snow**: Even June can have snow above 9,000ft — check CalTrans for pass conditions
- **Fire**: Check current Inyo NF restrictions religiously
- **Fishing**: CA fishing license required; check DFW for season dates and special regs on wild trout waters

### Alabama Hills Specifics (High Priority Target)
- BLM Lone Pine Field Office — free dispersed camping
- No permit needed; 14-day limit
- Iconic arch and rock formations; movie road
- Easy 4Runner access; most roads passable stock
- Proximity to Whitney Portal (fishing + day hiking)
